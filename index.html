My name is Noah Ramirez, and I have a passion for writing, localizing, and marketing content. I truly enjoy the simple beauty of finding the right words — often the simplest ones — to describe something, be it an idea, a product, a movie synopsis, the feeling you get when a song inspires you, or the joy of giving advice, support, or consolation to someone in need. This passion was awakened when I began working on a "metadata project" at a digital marketing agency in Santiago, Chile. As part of the job, I had to read the plots and production notes of movies and television series, identify the most meaningful parts of that information, and condense and convey them to viewers. That was already a fantastic experience and job, but it became even better thanks to the second phase of work: localizing it for global audiences. Sitting at the crossroads between cultures gave me profound insight into how people around the world not only consume content, but how they can be sold on it — and, hopefully, fall in love with it. When I am not working to connect people and ideas or striving to get the most out of content (as well as the people and technologies that bring it to life), I enjoy running, playing soccer on the weekend, reading, shanking golf balls, badly managing fantasy football teams, e-learning and practicing coding, binge-listening true crime podcasts, and spending time with my beautiful family.
